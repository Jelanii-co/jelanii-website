JELANII ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits jelanii.co and joins The Reserve. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. We keep it direct — because that is how we operate.

01

What We Collect

When you visit jelanii.co or submit your email address to join The Reserve, we may collect:

  • Your email address — provided voluntarily when you request access to The Reserve
  • Basic usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and device information collected automatically via standard web analytics
  • IP address — collected automatically as part of standard web server logs

We do not collect payment information, physical addresses, or any sensitive personal data through this website at this time.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect exclusively to:

  • Send you emails about JELANII — collection previews, drop dates, early access windows, and brand updates as a member of The Reserve
  • Improve jelanii.co based on how visitors use the site
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will never sell your information. We will never share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your information exists in The Reserve, not in anyone else's hands.

03

Email Communications

By joining The Reserve, you consent to receive email communications from JELANII. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time — and when you do, we will remove you promptly.

We use Klaviyo to manage our email list and communications. Klaviyo stores your email address on our behalf. You can review Klaviyo's privacy practices at klaviyo.com/legal/privacy-notice.

04

Cookies and Tracking

jelanii.co may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors interact with the site. These are used solely to improve the site experience — not to track you across other websites or build advertising profiles.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to view the site.

05

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate jelanii.co and The Reserve:

  • Klaviyo — email list management and automated email delivery
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Google Fonts — typography delivery

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We select services that meet reasonable privacy standards and use them only as necessary to operate the site.

06

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your information
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from email communications at any time via the link in any email we send
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format (where applicable)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

07

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information.

We do not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, contact us at the address below.

08

International Visitors (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your personal data, and the right to object to or restrict processing.

Our legal basis for processing your email address is your consent — given when you submit your email address to join The Reserve. You may withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing.

09

Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain a member of The Reserve. If you unsubscribe, we will remove your email address from our active list promptly. We may retain suppressed email addresses to honor unsubscribe requests — this prevents you from being accidentally re-added.

10

Children's Privacy

jelanii.co is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11

We may update this Privacy Policy as JELANII grows and our practices evolve. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of jelanii.co after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
